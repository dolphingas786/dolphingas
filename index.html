Who we are

An energy partner with a long-term outlook.

Dolphin Gas (Private) Limited is a Pakistan-based LPG business focused on storage, filling, supply and customer service. Our 50 MT plant at Mohri patak, Fateh Jang road, Islamabad, 44000, Pakistan, supports our ambition to serve domestic, commercial and industrial market requirements.

We believe lasting customer confidence comes from responsible handling, disciplined operations, transparent business practices and reliable communication.
